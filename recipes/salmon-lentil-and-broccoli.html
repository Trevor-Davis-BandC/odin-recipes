Salmon with Lentils & Roasted Broccoli

Lots of nourishing ingredients. Satisfying hearty meal that is iron rich.

Ingredients

  • 4 salmon fillets (about 5–6 oz each)
  • 1 cup dry green or brown lentils, rinsed
  • 1 large head broccoli, cut into floretsl
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 lemons
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil, divided
  • 2 cups low-sodium vegetable or chicken broth
  • 1 teaspoon paprika
  • ½ teaspoon dried thyme
  • ½ teaspoon black pepper
  • ½ teaspoon salt, or to taste
  • 2 tablespoons chopped fresh parsley

Steps

  1. Cook the lentils: Add the rinsed lentils and broth to a saucepan. Bring to a boil, then reduce the heat and simmer for about 20–25 minutes, or until tender. Drain any excess liquid and set aside.
  2. Prepare the oven: Preheat the oven to 400°F (200°C).
  3. Season the broccoli: Place the broccoli florets on a baking sheet. Toss with 1 tablespoon olive oil, half of the minced garlic, a little salt and black pepper. Roast for about 15–20 minutes, until tender and lightly browned.
  4. Season the salmon: Pat the salmon fillets dry. Rub them with the remaining olive oil and garlic, paprika, thyme, salt and black pepper. Squeeze the juice of 1 lemon over the salmon.
  5. Cook the salmon: Place the salmon on a lined baking sheet and bake for approximately 12–15 minutes, or until it flakes easily with a fork and reaches 145°F (63°C) in the thickest part.
  6. Finish the lentils: Stir the chopped parsley and a squeeze of fresh lemon juice into the warm lentils. Taste and adjust the seasoning if needed.
  7. Serve: Spoon the lentils onto each plate and top with the salmon. Add the roasted broccoli alongside and garnish with fresh parsley and wedges from the remaining lemon.
