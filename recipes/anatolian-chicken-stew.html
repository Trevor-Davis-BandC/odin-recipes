Anatolian Chicken Stew

A one pot stew from the Turkish part of the Mediterranean.

Ingredients

  • 1/2 Chicken (1/2 of a 4 pound chicken), cut into 2” pieces
  • salt and pepper
  • 2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  • 1 small red onion, chopped
  • 1 red bell pepper, sliced into thin strips
  • 1 garlic clove, minced
  • 1 15 ounce can crushed tomatoes
  • 1 cup chicken broth
  • 1 cup water
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 1 tbsp oregano
  • 1/2 cup green olives, pitted
  • 2 tbsp capers, chopped if too big
  • 1/4 cup fresh parsley

Steps

  1. Season the chicken with salt and pepper
  2. Heat dutch oven and add olive oil.
  3. Add chicken and cook on every side until golden brown. Remove chicken for later.
  4. Add the crushed tomatoes, chicken broth, water, bay leaves and oregano. Turn heat to high.
  5. Add chicken back to the pot making sure chicken is covered with liquied. Bring to a boil.
  6. Simmer for 15 more minutes or until chicken is done and serve with capers, green olives, and fresh parsley.
